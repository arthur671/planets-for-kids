# Privacy Policy for Planets for Kids

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Planets for Kids does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere.
- It does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts or your files.
- It contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services.
- It has no accounts and no in-app purchases.
- Nothing you do in the app is saved.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to delete or to request.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/arthur671/planets-for-kids/issues
